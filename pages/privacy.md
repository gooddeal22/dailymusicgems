Daily Music Gems is a small, independent music site. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking cookies. This page explains what little data is involved when you visit.

## What we collect

**Visit statistics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pieces people read. It does not use cookies, does not follow you across other websites, and does not build a profile of you.

**Server logs.** Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and block abuse. We do not use this to identify individual visitors.

**Email.** If you write to us, we keep your message and address only to reply and for our records. We do not sell or share them.

## Third-party services

**Fonts.** Text fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

**Music players.** Spotify, YouTube and SoundCloud players are never loaded until you click play. Once you do, that service may set cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy. A plain "Open in" link is always provided if you prefer to leave the page instead.

**Social links.** Links to TikTok and other sites take you off Daily Music Gems. Their policies apply there.

## Sponsored content

Anything paid for or provided by an artist, label or brand is clearly marked as sponsored on the page.

## Your choices

You can block cookies and scripts in your browser at any time, and the site will still work. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email partnerships@dailymusicgems.xyz.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
