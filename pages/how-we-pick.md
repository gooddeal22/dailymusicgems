Daily Music Gems is an independent music site run by Esau. It has two parts: curated playlists and daily picks, and longer writing about artists and albums.

## Gem of the Day

Each gem is a song or release we think deserves more ears. Picks are based on taste, not on promotion. Every gem links to the official stream on Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud. We never host the audio ourselves, and we do not publish lyrics.

## Writing

The writing covers style analyses, discography deep-dives, artist evolution pieces and album reviews. Reviews are honest opinions. A kind word is never a trade for coverage.

## Sponsored content

We never sell music promotion. Picks and reviews cannot be bought. If a post ever involves a paid partnership, free product or any other material connection, it is clearly marked as sponsored on the page, in line with advertising disclosure rules.
