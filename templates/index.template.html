03 · About
01

Daily Music Gems started from a simple frustration: most music writing online is either a reflexive hot take or a press-release rewrite. Neither actually engages with how the music is made.

02

This site is my attempt to do something slower and more useful — treat every artist's style as something worth taking apart carefully: their choices in production, structure, vocal delivery, arrangement, and how those choices change from one release to the next. The goal is criticism that's constructive, even when it's critical.

03

Alongside the writing, I'm building out playlists across Spotify, YouTube Music, and SoundCloud — the same ear applied to curation instead of prose.

04

The site is brand new. Some things above are still marked "coming soon" or as honest placeholders — nothing here is pretending to be finished. Check back as it fills in.

— Esau, founder & writer
